Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026. This policy explains what Calora collects, why, where it is stored and the choices you have. Calora is operated from Australia and aims to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are stored securely by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.

Profile details you enter: name, sex, age, height, weight, activity level, goal and calorie target.

Health and lifestyle logs you enter: meals and their nutrition, water intake and weight entries.

AI usage records: the time and type (text or photo) of each AI estimate, used only to apply fair-use limits.

We do not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and we do not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

To run the app: calculate your calorie target, show your daily summary, history and progress, and keep your data in sync across devices.

To protect the service: for example, daily limits on AI estimates.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

AI meal estimates

When you use Describe or Photo, the text you type or the photo you take is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce a nutrition estimate.

Calora does not store your meal photos. Only the foods you choose to save are kept in your log.

AI estimates are approximate. Always check and adjust them before saving.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a public food database, to look up the product. No personal details are sent.

Subscriptions and payments

Calora Premium subscriptions are bought through your Apple Account. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) to confirm whether your account has an active subscription. RevenueCat receives your Calora account ID, the subscription you chose and its status (for example started, renewed, cancelled or expired), and tells our server so Premium features can be unlocked. This information is used only to provide your subscription.

Account emails

Account emails (sign-up confirmation and password reset) are sent from calora.app.help@gmail.com using Google's email service.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Calora reads steps, walking and running distance, active energy, sleep and workouts from Apple Health to show your activity and sleep.

This Apple Health data is read on your iPhone only. It is not uploaded to our servers, not shared with third parties or our AI provider, and never used for advertising or sold.

Calora does not write to Apple Health. You can change or remove access at any time in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Calora.

Where your data is stored

Your account and logs are stored with our database provider, Supabase, in its Sydney, Australia region.

Some service providers, such as our AI provider, may process data outside Australia (for example in the United States). We only send what is needed for the feature you use.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Database rules ensure each account can only access its own data, and secret keys are kept on our servers, never in the app.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly if a problem is found.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active.

You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, profile, meals, water, weight and AI usage records. Backups held by our providers are removed on their normal schedule.

Your choices and rights

You can view and correct your details in the app at any time (Profile → Edit details).

You can ask for a copy of your data or ask a privacy question by emailing calora.support@gmail.com.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Age

Calora is intended for people aged 16 and over.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will update the date above, and tell you in the app about important changes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: calora.support@gmail.com
